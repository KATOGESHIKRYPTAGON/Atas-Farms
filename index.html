About Atas Farms

Atas Farms is a locally owned agricultural enterprise based in Machakos County, Kenya. We are committed to producing fresh, natural, and high-quality farm products for homes, businesses, and institutions across the country.

Our farm specializes in fresh fruits, natural honey, and healthy livestock, using sustainable and ethical farming practices that protect the environment and support local communities.
